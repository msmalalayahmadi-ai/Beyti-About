OUR STORY

A Legacy of Flavor

Since 1945, we've been bringing the heart of Turkish to every plate we serve.

EST. 1945

From Tuscany to Your
Table

Beyti bagan as a dream shared by the Turkish family - to bring the authentic flavors of their Tuscan homeland to America. What started as a small trattoria has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution.

Three generations later, we continue to honor our roots. Every recipe tells a story, every ingredient is carefully selected, and every guest becomes part of our extended family. Our commitment to quality and authenticity has remained unchanged since day one.
